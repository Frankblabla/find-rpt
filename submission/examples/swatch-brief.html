New collaboration: Audemars Piguet x Swatch

This report was selected by the user; the requested ticker is not verified in the source.[source]

ZKB sees several indications that the Audemars Piguet x Swatch collaboration could be another marketing masterstroke from Swatch and considers the frequently cited brand-dilution risk low.[source]

Key points

Rating is Market Perform.[source]

Reasons and context

After online speculation following a marketing teaser, Swatch Group officially confirmed the partnership with independent brand Audemars Piguet last Saturday under the name "Royal Pop", launching on 16 May. No executive names or management conversation reported.[source]

Notes and uncertainties

Earlier collaborations, the MoonSwatch with Omega (March 2022, retail from CHF 250) and Blancpain X Swatch (September 2023, retail from CHF 375), significantly boosted demand for the original models as well.[source]

ZKB estimates the MoonSwatch contributed around CHF 250 mn of revenue in 2022 (3.3% of the Group) and, together with Blancpain X Swatch, around CHF 450 mn in 2023.[source]

Besides design, the price range in particular is likely to play a decisive role in the success of the new collaboration.[source]

The Royal Pop watch combines elements of AP's Royal Oak with the 1986 Pop Swatch, which clips into a case with a wide strap, chain and other accessories.[source]

The collaborations sparked desire to own the original mechanical watch, priced from roughly CHF 25'000 at AP (ZKB estimate), made brand history accessible to a younger generation and generated enormous media visibility.[source]
