Newsflash Swatch Group (UHR) – New collaboration: Audemars Piguet x Swatch

This report was selected by the user; the requested ticker is not verified in the source.[source]

ZKB rates Swatch Group Market Perform and sees several indications that the Audemars Piguet x Swatch collaboration could be another marketing masterstroke, with price range likely decisive for its success.[source]

Key points

Rating: Market Perform. Sector: Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods.[source]

No price target, rating change or estimate revision is stated in this newsflash; only current key data for 2025, 2026E and 2027E is printed.

Reasons and context

After intense online speculation following a Swatch marketing teaser, Swatch Group officially confirmed last Saturday a partnership with independent brand Audemars Piguet (not part of the Group) under the name 'Royal Pop', launching on 16 May. No executive names or management conversation reported.[source]

Notes and uncertainties

ZKB estimates the MoonSwatch's revenue contribution at around CHF 250 mn in 2022 (3.3% of the Group) and, together with Blancpain X Swatch, around CHF 450 mn in 2023.[source]

ZKB concludes the collaborations sparked desire to own the original mechanical watch (roughly CHF 25'000 at AP, ZKB estimate), made brand history accessible to a younger generation and generated enormous media visibility; it considers brand-dilution risk low.[source]

The watch combines elements of AP's Royal Oak with the 1986 Pop Swatch, which can be clipped into a case with a wide strap, chain and other accessories.[source]
